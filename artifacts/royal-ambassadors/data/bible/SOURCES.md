# Bible text sources

The bundled King James Version, American Standard Version, and World English
Bible JSON files are from [thiagobodruk/bible](https://github.com/thiagobodruk/bible/tree/093b4727bf632181c9e096f4cf4141cc387371d0):

- [`en_kjv.json`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thiagobodruk/bible/093b4727bf632181c9e096f4cf4141cc387371d0/json/en_kjv.json)
- [`en_asv.json`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thiagobodruk/bible/093b4727bf632181c9e096f4cf4141cc387371d0/json/en_asv.json)
- [`en_web.json`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thiagobodruk/bible/093b4727bf632181c9e096f4cf4141cc387371d0/json/en_web.json)

The upstream repository's README states that Bible texts remain the property of
their respective owners; the repository's MIT license applies to its code, not
as a blanket license for every translation. Only the three editions selected
for this app are bundled. NIV, NKJV, ESV, and NLT are not included.
